**Recall 1.0.0: early access.** Search the pages you've visited by describing what you remember. Everything stays on your computer.

### Install

1. Download **`recall-1.0.0-chrome.zip`** below and unzip it to a folder you'll keep.
2. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, and pick the unzipped folder.
3. Read the welcome page and click **Agree and start**. Optionally allow Recall to read pages for full-text search.

Then browse normally. To search, click the Recall icon, press **Alt+Shift+R**, or type **`rc` + space** in the address bar.

### What's in 1.0.0

- Natural-language search over your browsing history, using an on-device AI model (MiniLM) plus typo-tolerant keyword matching
- Understands dates in your query ("last week", "in March") and has filters for date, website and saved pages
- Imports your recent Chrome history (titles and addresses). Pages become fully searchable when you visit them.
- Privacy controls: nothing is recorded until you agree, Incognito and sensitive sites are skipped, and you can exclude sites, pause, export, delete anything, or turn Recall off entirely
- No servers, accounts, analytics or network requests. The model is bundled.

### Good to know

- This build isn't from the Chrome Web Store, so it won't auto-update. Watch this repo's releases.
- When Recall reaches the store, the store version installs as a separate extension. Your early-access index stays with this copy and does not carry over (you can export it as JSON from Settings).
